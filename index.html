Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about installation and compatibility.

How do I install the extension ZIP in Chrome? ▾
1. Download the extension ZIP file.
2. Unzip the folder on your computer.
3. Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions.
4. Enable Developer mode (top right toggle).
5. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder. Done!
Which AI generators are supported? ▾
It generates tailored prompts for Midjourney v6.1, Flux (Schnell & Dev), Stable Diffusion XL, DALL-E 3, and Leonardo.ai.
Is my image uploaded to external third-party servers? ▾
No. The extension operates in your browser sandbox following strict Manifest V3 protocols with zero third-party tracking or remote image caching.
Does it work on Instagram, Pinterest, or Behance? ▾
Yes! The built-in Area Screen Snip tool allows you to easily capture and reverse-engineer prompts from any protected or dynamically rendered website.
