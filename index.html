Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions regarding reverse prompt generation and privacy.

What is a reverse image prompt generator? ▾
A reverse image prompt generator uses computer vision algorithms to examine an existing photograph, render, or artwork, and converts its composition, subject matter, lighting, and camera optics into a structured text prompt for generative AI tools like Midjourney and Flux.
How do I install the extension ZIP in Google Chrome? ▾
1. Download the extension ZIP file.
2. Unzip the archive into a folder on your computer.
3. In Google Chrome, go to chrome://extensions.
4. Enable the Developer mode toggle at the top right.
5. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
How does aspect ratio detection work? ▾
The extension calculates the ratio between the image natural width and height. If the image is 3840x2160, it automatically computes 16:9 and appends --ar 16:9 to the Midjourney prompt output.
Are my uploaded images stored or sent to remote servers? ▾
No. The extension operates completely inside your browser following Manifest V3 standards. No images or prompts are saved on third-party servers.
